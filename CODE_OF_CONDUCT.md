# Code of Conduct

English | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our Standard

DSH WhaleConsole welcomes constructive participation from people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, discuss ideas and behavior rather than attacking people, give actionable feedback, and assume good faith while remaining open to correction.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publication of another person's private information, and knowingly submitting malicious code or concealed credentials are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews, project-managed community spaces, and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or reject contributions, limit participation, or ban accounts when behavior threatens the safety or usefulness of the project community. Responses should be proportionate, documented where appropriate, and focused on protecting participants and the project.

Use GitHub's content-reporting tools for sensitive concerns and avoid reposting the affected content. For a repository moderation request that does not contain private details, notify the maintainer in the affected thread or through the most relevant issue form. Reports made in good faith will be handled as privately as the available platform tools permit.

## Privacy

Do not include credentials, personal logs, home-directory paths, private messages, or unnecessary identifying information in conduct reports. Security vulnerabilities should follow the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md), not this conduct process.
