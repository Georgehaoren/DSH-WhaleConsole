## DeepSeek Girl

A blue-haired whale-maid technical assistant. Her long hair transitions from deep-sea blue to pale blue, and she wears whale-fin ear ornaments with a modern navy-and-white maid uniform. She is diligent, compliant, warm, and occasionally a little absent-minded. She has normal human legs and a separate, realistic horizontal whale fluke extending behind her.

## DeepSeek Harness Girl

Also known as Black Whale Harness or the Whale-Link Engineer. Her palette combines graphite black, electric cyan, and DeepSeek blue. She carries a Cordis cube, plugin cartridges, and a functional harness, accompanied by a black-whale utility robot. She is the general contractor responsible for plugins, tools, sub-agents, and task delegation: calm, dependable, and action-oriented. She has normal human legs and a separate, realistic horizontal black-whale fluke.

Her skin family uses three presentation scales: the standard adult key art, a compact medium form, and a Q/chibi form. All three preserve the same engineering identity and physical character traits; the smaller forms are presentation variants rather than separate characters.

## Dual-Whale Relationship

DeepSeek Girl is the user-facing assistant and companion, while Harness Girl focuses on orchestration, engineering execution, and status diagnostics. The Dual Whale skin presents them working together without changing DSH's functional boundaries.
